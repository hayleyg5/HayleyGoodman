My Story

Hi, I’m Hayley! A software developer based in Ottawa, Canada, with a passion for UI/UX design and building intuitive digital experiences. After graduating from the University of Ottawa with a Bachelor’s in Computer Engineering, I joined a military tech startup as an Embedded Software Architect.

As their first engineering hire, I contributed to early-stage product architecture, designed prototype flows, and implemented the full user interface for our core Android application.

Outside of engineering, I love exploring new places and cultures. In the past year alone, I’ve traveled across Europe, North America, and the Caribbean—always taking inspiration from design trends and architectures along the way.

Art and creative expression have always been central to my work. Check out my favorite paintings in my portfolio, review my resume, or reach out to connect!

When I’m away from my desk, you'll usually find me golfing or skiing at Mont Tremblant.
